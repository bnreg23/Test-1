# LSAT Flaw Finder

A study tool for reviewing LSAT questions you got wrong.

Live app: https://claude.ai/artifact/CGH9YaPqB5cArrp1s2G99L (runs inside claude.ai; no API key needed)

## How it works
1. **Review:** drop in screenshots of missed questions (from 7Sage or any practice test), paste them, or paste the text. Claude reads the stimulus, question, answer choices, and (when they're marked) your answer and the correct answer.
2. **Your thinking:** confirm the answers and write a sentence or two about how you reasoned. You also mark how sure you were.
3. **Find my flaw:** you get:
   - where *your* reasoning slipped, why the wrong answer was tempting, and what to do next time
   - the stimulus in plain English (conclusion, evidence, the key gap)
   - the right thinking process, step by step
   - every answer choice in plain English, with the trap it sets
   - a tutor chat for follow-up questions and original practice questions
4. **Weak spots:** your misses grouped by question type and by type of reasoning mistake, plus a study plan written from your own patterns.

Your log is saved privately to your claude.ai account. Only you can see it.

The whole app is the single file `index.html`.
